01 / Briefing

What is Alert5?

Alert5 is a cooperative, hex-based tactics game. You and your friends launch missions from the last aircraft carrier on Earth: The Phoenix.

One player takes the role of Gamemaster, designing challenging scenarios for the others to fly. Run them as one-off missions, or link them into an ongoing campaign to free the world from its robotic occupiers.
